# Contoso Aero 300 Warranty and Service Guide

The Aero 300 includes a two-year limited hardware warranty beginning on the
original purchase date. The warranty covers manufacturing defects but excludes
accidental damage, unauthorized repairs, liquid immersion, and operation
outside the published temperature range.

To request service, record the device serial number and purchase date, then
open a case with Contoso Support. Do not ship the device until support provides
a return authorization number.

Battery capacity loss is covered only when diagnostic testing shows less than
70 percent of original capacity during the first year.
