# Contoso Aero 300 Operations Manual

## Reset procedure

To reset the Aero 300, disconnect external power, hold the recessed Reset
button for 10 seconds, release it, and reconnect power. The status light flashes
amber during startup and turns solid green when the reset succeeds.

## Operating limits

Operate the Aero 300 between -10 C and 45 C. Store it between -20 C and 60 C.
The enclosure is rated IP54, so it resists limited dust and water spray but
must not be submerged.

## Battery

The internal battery provides up to 12 hours of typical operation. Recharge it
with the supplied 65 W USB-C adapter. A full charge normally takes 90 minutes.
